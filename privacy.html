Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Aether (“Aether,” “we,” “us”) is a personal manifestation and visualization app. This policy explains what information the app processes, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

The short version

  • Sign in with Apple is optional. Aether never receives your Apple password or payment-card information.
  • We do not sell personal information, show third-party ads, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • Durable app content can synchronize through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available.
  • Information needed to create personalized stories, images, affirmations, and narration is sent securely to cloud generation providers.

Information stored by Aether

Aether stores content and preferences needed to provide the app, including the display name you choose, personal context, intentions, generated visualization sessions, affirmation favorites, journal entries, dream entries, mood ratings, practice progress, reference photos, and narration preferences. Generated audio and other regenerable caches remain local to the device.

Private iCloud synchronization

When you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available, Aether uses Apple CloudKit to synchronize durable content through your private iCloud database. Apple controls that database under your iCloud account. Changes and deletions can propagate to your other devices. If iCloud is unavailable, the app can continue using its local store and will show the sync status in Settings.

Cloud generation

To create personalized guided visualizations, titles, images, affirmations, dream reflections, and narration, Aether sends the information needed for that request to our secured service and its generation providers, including OpenAI and Cartesia. Depending on the feature, this can include personal context, an intention or journal excerpt you chose to use for personalization, story text, narration settings, and reference images you supplied. When cloud generation is used:

  • The information is used to fulfill the feature you requested and operate or secure the service.
  • Your provider API credentials are not included in the app; requests pass through Aether’s secured service.
  • Aether does not use this content for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Those providers process API data under their applicable business and privacy terms. Network access is required for cloud-generated results.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Aether stores Apple’s app-specific account identifier and the name Apple supplies on first authorization. Aether converts that identifier into a non-reversible, app-specific identifier before using it to associate subscription access through RevenueCat. Sign in with Apple is not required for Apple’s private iCloud synchronization.

Subscriptions and purchases

Aether offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment details and determines trial eligibility. RevenueCat processes subscription status, product, renewal, cancellation, refund, billing, and entitlement information so Aether can provide and restore paid access. We do not receive your full payment-card information.

Diagnostics and product operation

Aether keeps an on-device diagnostics log to help you and support troubleshoot generation and playback problems. RevenueCat provides subscription lifecycle and revenue reporting. Aether does not currently use information for third-party advertising or cross-company tracking.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Aether schedules optional daily reminders locally on your device. We do not operate a push server and do not see whether or when reminders fire. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Aether is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your control

You can delete individual entries in the app, disconnect Sign in with Apple, or use “Delete Aether Data” to delete synchronized Aether content. Deleting synchronized content can remove it from your other devices. Deleting the app alone does not cancel a subscription or necessarily delete data in your private iCloud database. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Aether’s subscription center or your Apple ID settings.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, note the change in the app.
