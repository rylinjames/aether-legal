Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Aether (“Aether,” “we,” “us”) is a personal manifestation and visualization app. We designed Aether to be private by default: it works offline and your personal reflections stay on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

The short version

  • We do not require an account, email, or login.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • Your intentions, sessions, affirmations, journal entries, and dream entries are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, Aether stores the content you create — your name (a display name you choose), intentions, generated visualization sessions, affirmation favorites, gratitude journal entries, dream-journal entries, mood ratings, and practice streak — in local on-device storage (Apple SwiftData). This information never leaves your device unless you explicitly use an optional feature described below. Deleting the app removes this data.

Optional AI enhancement

Aether can generate guided visualization scripts and dream reflections entirely on-device. Some versions of the app may offer an optional AI-enhancement that improves these results by sending the relevant text you provide (for example, an intention or a dream description) to a third-party AI provider (OpenAI) for processing. When this optional feature is used:

  • Only the text needed to generate that result is sent, with no name, account, device identifier, or location attached.
  • The text is used solely to return the generated result and is not stored by us.
  • If the feature is unavailable or you do not enable it, the app falls back to fully on-device generation.

OpenAI’s handling of API data is governed by their own policies. If a build of Aether does not include this feature, no data is transmitted off your device at all.

Subscriptions and purchases

Aether offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time purchase through Apple’s App Store. Payments and any free-trial eligibility are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive or store your payment information. Apple’s processing of purchases is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Aether schedules optional daily reminders locally on your device. We do not operate a push server and do not see whether or when reminders fire. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Aether is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your control

Because your data lives on your device, you are in control: you can delete individual entries within the app, or remove all app data by deleting the app. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in your Apple ID settings.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, note the change in the app.
