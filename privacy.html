About this policy

Delia (“Delia,” “we,” “us”) is a personal manifestation and visualization app. This policy explains what information the app processes, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

The short version

  • Sign in with Apple is optional. Delia never receives your Apple password or payment-card information.
  • We do not sell personal information, show third-party ads, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • Durable app content can synchronize through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available.
  • If you choose Sign in with Apple, Delia can securely back up and restore your durable Delia content through your account.
  • Information needed to create personalized stories, images, affirmations, and narration is sent securely to cloud generation providers.

Information stored by Delia

Delia stores content and preferences needed to provide the app, including the display name you choose, personal context, intentions, generated visualization sessions, affirmation favorites, journal entries, dream entries, mood ratings, practice progress, reference photos, and narration preferences. Generated audio and other regenerable caches remain local to the device.

Private iCloud synchronization

When you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available, Delia uses Apple CloudKit to synchronize durable content through your private iCloud database. Apple controls that database under your iCloud account. Changes and deletions can propagate to your other devices. If iCloud is unavailable, the app can continue using its local store and will show the sync status in Settings.

Account backup and restoration

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Delia creates an account session using Apple’s app-specific identifier. Delia stores a private account archive so you can restore your durable Delia content after signing in again on another device. The archive can include your display name, personal context, intentions, generated stories and images, affirmation favorites, journal and dream entries, mood ratings, practice progress, reference photos, and preferences. Generated audio and other regenerable caches remain on the device.

The archive is transmitted securely and stored in private Delia infrastructure operated through Cloudflare. It is used only to provide account restoration, synchronization, security, and support for the app. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Cloud generation

To create personalized guided visualizations, titles, images, affirmations, dream reflections, and narration, Delia sends the information needed for that request to our secured service and its generation providers, including OpenAI and Cartesia. Depending on the feature, this can include personal context, an intention or journal excerpt you chose to use for personalization, story text, narration settings, and reference images you supplied. When cloud generation is used:

  • The information is used to fulfill the feature you requested and operate or secure the service.
  • Your provider API credentials are not included in the app; requests pass through Delia’s secured service.
  • Delia does not use this content for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Those providers process API data under their applicable business and privacy terms. Network access is required for cloud-generated results.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Delia uses Apple’s app-specific account identifier and the name Apple supplies on first authorization to establish and restore your Delia account. Delia also converts that identifier into a non-reversible, app-specific identifier before using it to associate subscription access through RevenueCat. Sign in with Apple is not required for Apple’s private iCloud synchronization.

Subscriptions and purchases

Delia offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment details and determines trial eligibility. RevenueCat processes subscription status, product, renewal, cancellation, refund, billing, and entitlement information so Delia can provide and restore paid access. We do not receive your full payment-card information.

Diagnostics and product operation

Delia keeps an on-device diagnostics log to help you and support troubleshoot generation and playback problems. RevenueCat provides subscription lifecycle and revenue reporting. Delia does not currently use information for third-party advertising or cross-company tracking.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Delia schedules optional daily reminders locally on your device. We do not operate a push server and do not see whether or when reminders fire. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Delia is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your control

You can delete individual entries in the app, disconnect Sign in with Apple, or use “Delete Delia Data” to permanently delete your local content, private iCloud content, and signed-in Delia account archive. Deleting synchronized content can remove it from your other devices. Deleting the app alone does not cancel a subscription or necessarily delete data in your private iCloud database. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Delia’s subscription center or your Apple ID settings.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, note the change in the app.
